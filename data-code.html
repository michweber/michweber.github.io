My research draws on a wide range of data, with two major survey programs playing an important role: the long-running NielsenIQ survey experiments in the United States and the Global Subjective Expectations Survey (GSES), launched with the BIS in 2025 and conducted jointly by the EEC and BIS since 2026. Together, these projects study how people form expectations and how those expectations relate to economic behavior.

The sections below provide data and materials from these projects, alongside other datasets used in my research.

Customized NielsenIQ Surveys

Since 2015, customized surveys have been fielded to the NielsenIQ consumer (Homescan) panel, a U.S. household panel that records purchases by scanning them at home. The surveys combine households’ stated expectations, their scanned purchases and the prices they paid, and randomized information treatments, which allows the analysis to move from correlation to causation.

EEC-BIS Global Subjective Expectations Survey (GSES)

Launched in 2025, this annual survey provides comparable evidence on households’ economic expectations across advanced and emerging economies. Its harmonized questionnaire enables cross-country comparisons of inflation expectations, perceptions of prices and wages, central bank knowledge, and information sources, while rotating special modules allow the survey to explore emerging economic questions. The survey is conducted by the European Expectations Center (EEC) in collaboration with the Bank for International Settlements (BIS). More data will be shared here soon.

Coverage32 countries, about 65% of the world’s population and over 85% of global GDP
MethodologyOnline survey panels with a harmonized sampling approach across waves. Samples are designed to be representative of each country’s adults aged 18–70 by age and gender, although they are not probability samples of the full population. Respondents are screened for age and attention, and the questionnaire is translated into each country’s official languages using a common protocol.
Distinctive featureHarmonized questionnaire across countries, with rotating special modules
FrequencyAnnual
